Appendix A

Context Cheat Sheet

The dbt Jinja names you'll reach for most often, grouped by purpose. The Where column uses these abbreviations: M = models/snapshots (and their hooks), T = generic tests, Mat = materializations, Op = run-operation and on-run-start/end, Y = YAML/project files. Members marked exec only return real values when execute is true.

Built-in macros you may override

By name: generate_schema_name, generate_alias_name, generate_database_name, ref/source (via builtins). Through dispatch (default__/postgres__): create_table_as, get_merge_sql, get_incremental_merge_sql, get_test_sql, test_unique, test_not_null, test_accepted_values, test_relationships, every dbt.* utility, and more.
